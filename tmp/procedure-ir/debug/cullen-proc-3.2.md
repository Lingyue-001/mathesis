The further emendation of ‘Era’ to ‘Obscuration’ is also helpful. The aim of this section is to find the sexagenary number (year-name) of the year now beginning. We do this by finding the sexagenary number of the first year of the current Obscuration, and then counting forward. The requisite data are provided in Table 3.1. First we cast out whole Origins, since all relevant conditions repeat in an Origin. Since an Origin is three Eras, we then establish which of the three Eras of the current Origin we are in. That tells us which of the three main columns (in the modern format – rows in the original) we should be using. The columns are named after Heaven, Earth and Man – Celestial, Terrestrial and Anthropic. Each column has in its first row the sexagenary number of its first year. Each column has 20 rows, containing the sexagenary numbers of the first year of each of the 20 Obscurations that make up the relevant Era. The rows are numbered 1 to 20 at the extreme left, just after the fourth column in which we are given the sexagenary number of the day on which the Obscurations in each row begin.
